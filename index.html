How sideways moves help

A move with unchanged h can cross a flat region to reach a state with a downhill exit (a shoulder). A flat local minimum may have no such exit. Immediate neighbors alone do not distinguish the two.

The consecutive limit prevents endless walks and cycles. A strictly improving move resets the sideways counter.
